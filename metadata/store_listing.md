### English (US)

  Local services, community support programs, and neighborhood events.
- **Full Description (4,000 chars max):**
  Esperanza is your dedicated community services and neighborhood portal, built to connect residents with essential programs, local organizations, and neighbors.

  Whether you are looking for local food assistance, neighborhood health resources, family workshops, or community events, Esperanza brings verified services to your fingertips.

  WHAT YOU CAN DO ON ESPERANZA:
  • Search Local Services: Easily browse verified community resources across health, food, education, housing, and social services.
